This is a balanced (reconciling) view. For the selected range each account satisfies the identity opening balance + all money in = all money out + ending balance, so every account node's left side (inflows) and right side (outflows) are equal by construction. Bands flow left→right: sources → the six WTNB accounts (kept separate) → uses. Everything is now captured — external revenue, the credit facility, inter-account transfers (movement among the Peak 10 accounts and to related entities), returns/reversals, and the opening & ending balances that absorb the period's change in cash. Inter-account transfers appear on both sides (one account's transfer-out is another's transfer-in), which is what makes the totals reconcile. Checks carry no payee and are grouped as Royalty & Owner Checks; ACH pay runs as AP / Vendor Pay Runs. Matched bounced-payment/return pairs are netted; unmatched ones show under Returns & Reversals.
